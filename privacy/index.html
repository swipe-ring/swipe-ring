Swipe Ring

Privacy Policy

Swipe Ring is a calm puzzle game about turning a wooden board and letting candy rings slip into a fountain. This policy explains, in plain words, which game records the app keeps on your device, what each one is for, when it is written and read, and how you can clear it whenever you like.

1What Swipe Ring keeps on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, Swipe Ring keeps a small set of game records in the app's own preferences on your iPhone. These records are:

  • Stars earned on each of the 120 levels, which also decide which levels and chapters are open to you.
  • Your glow balance, the soft light you collect and spend to reveal the next helpful step on a board.
  • A running count of rings you have dropped into the fountain, shown in the Ring Atlas.
  • Daily Ripple records: which days you solved the daily board and how many stars you earned on each of them, used for the seven-day row and your streak.
  • Your choices for the four switches in Settings: Music, Sound effects, Haptics and Fall preview.
  • A single flag that notes you have already seen the short guide shown on the first level.

That is the complete list. Each record is a plain number, a day label or an on/off value that belongs to the game itself. Together they take up only a few kilobytes, about the size of a short note.

The boards themselves, the artwork and the music are part of the app you installed, so they are the same for every player and are not tied to you in any way.

2Why each record is kept

Stars let the chapter map show your progress, unlock the next level and fill in the chapter trophies in the home garden as chapters are finished.

Glow is kept so that a hint you have earned is still there the next time you open the game. The ring count and daily records feed the numbers on the Ring Atlas and Daily Ripple screens, so you can see how far you have come.

The Settings switches are stored so the game sounds, feels and behaves the way you chose, every time you return. The guide flag simply keeps the first-level guide from appearing again once you have read it.

These records are used only to run the game for you. They are what lets Swipe Ring pick up exactly where you left the garden, with the same stars, the same glow and the same quiet settings.

3When records are written and read

Records are written at the moment something changes: when you finish a level, when glow is earned or spent, when you solve the Daily Ripple, when you flip a switch, or when you close the first-level guide.

They are read when the game opens and whenever a screen that shows them appears, such as the home garden, a chapter map, the Daily Ripple, the Ring Atlas or Settings. The Fall preview switch is checked each time you press and hold a ring on a board, and the sound switches are checked each time the game is about to play a chime or a splash.

Moves you make on a board, including undo steps, are kept in memory only while that board is open and are let go as soon as you leave it or start the board again.

4Where records live and how they are looked after

All game records stay inside Swipe Ring's own space on your iPhone and are protected by the security features of iOS, including the passcode and encryption of your device.

If your device backup settings include app data, the records travel with your regular iPhone backup in the same way as other app settings, and come back when that backup is restored.

5Emails you send to us

Settings includes a Contact Us row. Tapping it opens the mail app on your device with a new email addressed to camisang94@icloud.com. If no mail app is set up, the address is copied so you can paste it wherever you like.

When you write to us, we receive the email address you send from and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to read your note and reply to you, and we keep the conversation for as long as it is helpful for answering your question.

6Links that open in your browser

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use rows in Settings open the matching web pages in Safari. The Rate Swipe Ring row opens the App Store rating view provided by Apple. Each of these runs under the policies of the app or service that opens it.

7Your choices

  • Change any of the four switches in Settings at any time; the new choice is saved right away.
  • Use Reset progress in Settings to clear stars, cleared levels, Daily Ripple records, the ring count and the guide flag, and to return your glow to its starting amount. Your switch choices stay as they are.
  • Delete Swipe Ring from your iPhone to remove every record the game keeps.

You can do all of this from inside the game or your device, without needing to contact us first. If you would like help with any of these steps, just write to us and we will walk you through it.

8Updates and contact

If Swipe Ring gains new features that change what the game keeps, this policy will be updated to describe them, and the updated version will be published here.

Questions, ideas or requests about your game records are always welcome at camisang94@icloud.com. We read every email and will do our best to help.
