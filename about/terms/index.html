1About the game

Swipe Ring is a puzzle game. Candy rings hang from pegs on a round wooden board; you turn the board to the left or right, or tap a ring to turn its gap either way, and rings slip off when a gap faces up. The goal on every board is to drop all the rings into the fountain below, keeping the gold ring for last.

The game includes four chapters of thirty levels each, a Daily Ripple board that changes every day, a Ring Atlas that explains every kind of ring, and a Settings screen for sound, haptics and the fall preview.

Levels open one after another as you clear them, and each chapter wakes up once the chapter before it is complete. You can replay any level you have already cleared as often as you like to improve your stars.

3Glow and in-game rewards

Glow is a soft light you collect inside the game. You begin with five, earn one more the first time you finish a level with three stars, and earn three more the first time you solve each day's Daily Ripple. Spending one glow reveals a suggested next step on the current board.

Stars follow the par shown on each board: matching the par earns three stars, staying within two moves of it earns two, and any other finish earns one. Undo is always free, and every level can be replayed.

Glow, stars and streaks exist only inside Swipe Ring. They have no value outside the game and cannot be exchanged, transferred or traded. Reset progress in Settings, or removing the app, returns them to their starting state.
